Privacy policy

Your stream stays under your control.

Overlay Stream does not require an account and does not include ads or analytics.

Effective September 26, 2026

Information handled by the app

The app uses camera and microphone data to create the preview, recordings, and live stream you request. When you start a live stream, that media is sent to the streaming server you selected.

Stream keys, server addresses, and overlay addresses are held in app memory for the current session. The app does not save them to persistent storage or send them to a developer-operated server.

Recordings and Photos

If you create a test recording, the app saves the completed video to your Photos library after you grant permission. You control and can delete that recording through the Photos app.

Overlay and streaming services

The app loads the overlay address you provide and connects to the streaming destination you choose. Those third parties may receive your IP address, device or connection information, media, and any information you intentionally send to them. Their own terms and privacy policies apply.

  • Meta Privacy Policy

Permissions

Camera and microphone access are required for preview, recording, and streaming. Add-only Photos access is used only to save a test recording. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings.

Data collection and retention

The developer does not collect or retain personal data through the app. Because the app has no developer account or cloud storage, there is no developer-held account data to delete.

Children

Overlay Stream is not directed to children under 13. Streaming services may impose additional age requirements.

Policy changes

This policy may change when the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support page. Do not include stream keys or other credentials in a support request.
